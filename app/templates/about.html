About the platform

ClearGrade

Assessment with clarity and integrity.

ClearGrade is a proctored online assessment platform built for lecturers who need to run a secure, invigilated test without asking students to install anything beyond a browser. Each course runs on its own database, its own students, and its own settings - one lecturer's exam never shares data with another's, even when they're hosted on the same platform.

Set up your own course What this system records

What it does

Students register, verify their email, and sit a timed paper in a locked-down, full-screen browser window with camera-based proctoring. Every interruption - leaving full screen, losing the camera feed, a flagged incident - is timestamped and logged for the lecturer to review alongside the submission itself, so grading decisions are backed by evidence rather than guesswork.

Your data, isolated

Every course gets its own database connection - a lecturer's own, or one the platform provisions for them. Nothing is pooled or shared across courses, so one lecturer's students, submissions, and proctoring evidence are never visible to another course, even when both run on the same ClearGrade deployment.

Where it started

ClearGrade began as a course tool at the University of Zambia (UNZA) and has since grown into a platform any lecturer can sign up to and configure for their own course, with their own connection details, independent of where it was first built.
